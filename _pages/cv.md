Ph.D. Candidate, Department of Computer Science, Yonsei University. Ph.D. expected February 2027. Available for a postdoctoral position from March 2027.

Research Interests
======
Database systems and vector data management: graph-based approximate nearest neighbor search (HNSW), index maintenance under updates, robustness to out-of-distribution queries, hardware-conscious index layouts, retrieval-augmented generation, and machine learning for database tuning.
